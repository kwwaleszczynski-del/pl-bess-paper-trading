Battery paper trading on the Polish day-ahead market

Every morning a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery forecasts tomorrow's day-ahead prices, plans when to charge and discharge, and publishes the plan before the 12:00 gate closure. The next day the plan is settled on the published SDAC clearing prices and added to a track record that is never recomputed.

Paper trading only. No bids or offers are submitted to any market or exchange: a plan is a file, and its result is computed from public prices. This is a research and portfolio project, not investment, trading or financial advice, and past or simulated results say nothing reliable about future results.

What is known at the decision

The forecast for delivery day D uses only information published by about 07:00 Polish time on D-1. The plan is issued by the first scheduled run that starts between then and the 12:00 gate (five attempts between 07:10 and 10:40 CET, 08:10 and 11:40 CEST); a later run does not see more, because the inputs are cut at fixed times. Method

Forecast

One gradient boosting model per quantile (10 %, 50 %, 90 %; scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor with pinball loss) predicts the price of every quarter-hour of D from 26 as-of features: time of day, weekday, holidays, season, the prices of D-1, D-2 and D-7 at the same time, the shape of D-1 (mean, minimum, maximum, spread, night, midday and evening levels, share of negative prices) and PSE system data up to 06:00 on D-1. It is trained on the 365 preceding delivery days. Until 30.09.2025 the SDAC products were hourly, so the forecast and the schedule are hourly on those days.

Schedule

A mixed-integer linear program (HiGHS) maximises the energy revenue at the median forecast minus a degradation cost of 30 PLN per MWh discharged, with 87 % round-trip efficiency, state of charge between 5 % and 95 %, 50 % at the start and the end of every day, no simultaneous charge and discharge and at most 1.5 cycles per day. The plan is a fixed schedule (price-taking quantities), as if submitted to SDAC without price limits.

Settlement and benchmarks

Every strategy is settled on the SDAC clearing price of the Polish zone with the same battery and costs:

  • Forecast + MILP: the live strategy.
  • Yesterday's prices: the schedule that would have been optimal on D-1, known at the decision.
  • Fixed hours: every day, at full power within the limits. The hours come from the average price profile of June 2023 to June 2024, before the first backtest day.
  • Perfect foresight: the schedule optimised on the clearing prices themselves, an upper bound that cannot be traded.

Live record

  • The plan for D is written to the public track-record branch before 12:00 on D-1, with the model inputs and the PSE 5-day plan as seen at that moment. A plan is never replaced; after the gate no plan is made.
  • Each day is settled once, when its prices are published, and appended to ledger.csv. Rows are never rewritten, also when the code changes later. A day without a plan counts as no trade (0 PLN).
  • The record starts on the first published plan; the backtest is shown separately and never merged into it.

Where the backtest is not exactly as-of

  • PSE system data: the API serves only the latest revision, so the backtest sees corrected values where the live run sees first estimates. They enter only as day and night averages.
  • Models are refitted weekly in the backtest and daily in the live run.
  • The first backtest models (June 2024 to June 2025) are partly trained on the hourly RCE of the legacy PSE export, the price of the day-ahead market before the balancing market reform of 14.06.2024.
  • The model settings were fixed before the backtest and not tuned on it, but this project was designed after the period it tests.

Limitations

  • 1 MW is assumed too small to move prices; fees, imbalance, grid tariffs, taxes and financing are ignored.
  • Only the day-ahead market: no intraday trading, balancing services or capacity market revenue.
  • A real trader would submit price-dependent bids; a fixed schedule is a simple, conservative stand-in.
